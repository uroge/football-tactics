# Football Tactics

A personal companion for viewing football with tactical graphics on an Android TV.

## Language

**Analysis session**  
The user-authorized interval during which the companion can analyze playback and display annotations.

**Playback app**  
The separate TV app that displays the video the user is watching.

**Feasibility gate**  
The physical-device test of simultaneous authorized capture, visible overlay, and continued remote control of playback.

**Track ID**  
A temporary identifier for a detected subject across frames in a continuous camera shot.

**Overlay**  
A layer of graphics shown over playback. An annotation is one graphic within that layer.
